Privacy Policy for Docs & PDF Editor

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EEB Consultancy LLC ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with the Docs & PDF Editor mobile application (the "App").

Docs & PDF Editor allows users to create, edit, scan, convert, organize, and manage documents, spreadsheets, presentations, PDF files, and related content.

The App is designed to process documents primarily on your device. Certain optional services, including advertising and in-app purchases, involve third-party providers as described below.

1. Scope of This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes:

  • Information and files processed by the App.
  • How documents are stored and managed.
  • Device permissions used by the App.
  • Advertising and related data processing.
  • Subscription and purchase processing.
  • Your privacy choices and rights.

The App does not require account registration to use its core document features.

2. App Features

The App provides tools for:

  • Creating and editing documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
  • Viewing and annotating PDF files.
  • Scanning paper documents.
  • Converting supported file formats.
  • Organizing and managing files.
  • Accessing optional Premium features through subscriptions.
  • Accessing eligible file conversions by watching rewarded advertisements.

3. Documents and User Content

Depending on the features you use, the App may access and process:

  • Documents and text files.
  • Spreadsheets and workbook data.
  • Presentations and slides.
  • PDF documents.
  • Images selected from your device.
  • Scanned document content.
  • File names and organizational metadata.

This content is processed to provide the document-related features you request.

We do not use the contents of your documents for advertising personalization or sell your document content to advertisers.

4. Local Processing and Storage

The App is designed to perform its core document operations locally on your device.

  • Documents are created and edited locally.
  • Scanned documents are processed within the App.
  • Supported file conversions are performed locally.
  • Files are organized and managed within the device's storage environment.

The App does not require uploading your documents to servers operated by EEB Consultancy LLC to use its core document features.

Advertising and purchase-related network requests are separate from document processing.

5. Device Permissions

Camera

Camera access may be requested when you choose to scan paper documents or capture content.

Photos

Photos access may be requested to import images or save exported files where applicable.

Files and Document Picker

The App uses supported iOS file selection features to access files you choose to import or manage.

Permissions can be managed through your device settings.

6. Account Registration

The App does not require users to create an account or provide a username and password to access its core functionality.

We do not provide account-based document storage on our own servers as part of the App's core features.

7. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded advertisements.

Users may choose to watch a rewarded advertisement to access an eligible file conversion without purchasing a subscription.

Google and its advertising partners may process information associated with advertising delivery, including:

  • Device information and operating system details.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information.
  • Advertising identifiers, where available and permitted.
  • Advertisement interactions and performance information.
  • Diagnostic information related to advertising services.

This information may be used by Google and its partners for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measuring performance, preventing fraud, and maintaining advertising services. Depending on applicable settings, consent, and legal requirements, advertising data may also be used for personalization.

Where required, advertising personalization or tracking is subject to applicable consent requirements and Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework.

Users can manage applicable tracking permissions through their device's privacy settings.

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional. Users may instead access eligible Premium functionality through an available subscription.

Google's privacy practices are described at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

8. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

The App offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple's in-app purchase system using StoreKit.

Available subscription options may include weekly and yearly plans. Eligible users may receive a free trial with the weekly subscription.

Purchases, payment processing, subscription renewals, and billing are managed by Apple.

We do not receive or store your complete payment card details.

To provide Premium access, the App may process purchase-related information made available through Apple's purchase system, such as:

  • Purchased product identifiers.
  • Subscription status and expiration information.
  • Transaction identifiers.
  • Purchase verification and entitlement information.

This information is used to determine access to purchased features, restore eligible purchases, and manage subscription-related functionality.

Subscriptions can be managed or canceled through your Apple Account settings.

Apple's privacy practices are described at:

https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

9. Analytics and Diagnostics

The App and the third-party services integrated into it may process limited technical and diagnostic information necessary for their operation.

Advertising-related measurement and diagnostics may be processed by Google AdMob. Purchase-related information may be processed through Apple's services.

We do not use the contents of your documents for third-party behavioral analytics.

10. File Management and Retention

Files may remain stored locally on your device until you delete them, remove the App, or otherwise manage them through the App or your device's storage features.

Locally stored content may also be affected by device backups and operating system storage behavior.

Advertising and purchase-related information handled by third-party providers is subject to their respective retention practices.

11. Sharing and Exporting Files

The App allows users to share, export, print, or save supported files using available iOS features.

When you choose to share content with another application or storage provider, that provider's privacy practices apply to the information you share.

We do not control the handling of files after you voluntarily transfer them to an external service.

12. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to support secure local document handling and user-controlled file management.

However, no application, device, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend protecting your device, maintaining appropriate backups, and exercising care when sharing sensitive documents.

13. Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through an account registration system.

Third-party advertising services may process information according to their policies and applicable legal requirements.

If you believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided directly to us, please contact us.

14. International Data Processing

The App's core document functionality is designed to operate locally on your device.

However, third-party advertising and purchase services may process information in countries outside your country of residence.

Such processing is governed by the applicable provider's privacy practices and relevant legal requirements.

15. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about processing.

You may also:

  • Manage camera and photo permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Manage applicable tracking permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Delete locally stored documents through the App's available file-management features.
  • Manage subscriptions through your Apple Account.
  • Contact us regarding privacy-related requests.

Requests concerning data controlled independently by Apple or Google may need to be directed to those providers.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in App functionality, third-party services, legal requirements, or privacy practices.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the beginning of this document.
